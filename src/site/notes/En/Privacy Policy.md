## Web analytics with Umami

This website uses Umami, an open-source tool for measuring reach, which runs on our own server in Germany (stats.polyformal.de). No cookies are set, no data is stored in your browser and no IP addresses are stored.

We record the page visited, the referring page, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and the approximate country or region. To distinguish visits, an anonymous identifier is derived from your IP address and user agent. It changes every month and cannot be traced back to you. No data is passed on to third parties.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in analysing how our website is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Opt-out:** If your browser sends the "Do Not Track" signal, no data is recorded. You can enable it in your browser's privacy settings.
